### 🎯 What is FirstOffer?

**FirstOffer** is a platform built to help **students and freshers find jobs, internships and off-campus opportunities in one place.**

Instead of searching across multiple Telegram groups, LinkedIn posts, company career pages and job portals, FirstOffer brings relevant opportunities together in a searchable feed.

### ✨ What FirstOffer Provides

- 💼 **Fresher Jobs & Internships**
- 🎓 **Opportunities for different graduation batches**
- 🚀 **Full-Time & Off-Campus Opportunities**
- 🏢 **Opportunities from startups & established companies**
- 📩 **HR Email IDs** wherever available
- 📞 **HR / Recruiter Contact Numbers** wherever available
- 📝 **Direct Google Forms** shared for applications
- 🔗 **Direct Company Application Links**
- 🔎 **Search & Filter** by role, location, skills, etc.
- ✨ **Resume Keyword Matcher** to compare resume keywords with job descriptions
- ⚡ **Fresh opportunities added regularly**
